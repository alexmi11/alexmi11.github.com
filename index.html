I am a Hertz Fellow at MIT, working with Brent Minchew, Jeffrey Lang, and Jeffrey Hoffman. I research glacier mechanics and develop sensing, actuation, and power infrastructure for hard-to-reach environments including the cryosphere, oceans, and space. I apply a multidisciplinary approach that balances theory and experiments towards understanding complex physical and engineering systems. I have been fortunate to learn from teams at X, the Moonshot Factory (formerly Google[x]) (Early-stage sensing systems), NASA JPL (Mars Science Helicopter), DeepMind (Robotics), Microsoft Research (Applied Science Group), and Wing. Ask me for my CV and portfolio!

I am also a cellist!
