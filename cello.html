Cello!

I have played cello for the past 23 years, in both classical and non-classical styles. I enjoy bringing the cello to places where it is not normally heard, and have played in rock venues, outdoor spaces, Somerville Porchfest, and at a Seattle Mariners game. I currently study with Kee-Hyun Kim, and am grateful for past instruction from Peter Williams, Jonathan Butler, Carol Ou, and have given solo recitals in 2016, 2017, 2021, and 2025. I have been building a cello in the pantry for the past few years, and am currently shaping the maple back. At MIT, I have participated in the Emerson/Harris Program and Chamber Music Society, and am grateful for coaching from Marcus Thompson. Previously, I have been a member of the Brookline Symphony, have performed with the Northwest Mahler Festival, and enjoy playing in folk and bluegrass jam sessions. Reach out if you'd like to collaborate on a performance, writing, or recording project!
